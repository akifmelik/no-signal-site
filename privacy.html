No Signal Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 30, 2026

Overview

No Signal is a narrative mobile game. We aim to collect as little personal data as possible and keep the product focused on gameplay.

Information We Collect

No Signal does not require account creation to play.

The app may store gameplay state locally on your device, such as progress, settings, and unlock status.

Purchases

If you make in-app purchases, the transaction is handled by Apple or Google through their platform billing systems. We do not receive your full payment details.

Analytics and Diagnostics

The app may use basic diagnostics or analytics in future versions to improve stability, gameplay tuning, and purchase reliability. If third-party analytics or crash reporting tools are added, this policy should be updated accordingly.

How We Use Information

Any information collected is used to:

  • save and restore game progress
  • provide premium unlocks and restore purchases
  • improve app stability and user support

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Limited information may be shared with platform providers or service providers only as needed to operate purchases, hosting, or app functionality.

Children's Privacy

No Signal is not directed to children under 13.
