Support

Need help with No Signal?

For technical help, purchase issues, or bug reports, contact: akifmelik@gmail.com

Please include your device model, OS version, app version, and a short description of the issue.

Restore purchases

Open the store screen in the app and use the restore option if a previous purchase is not showing correctly.

No audio

Check your device volume, silent mode, and in-app audio toggles. Some haptics may still work even when sound is muted.

Progress issues

If progress does not resume correctly, mention the chapter and the last scene you reached in your support email.

Response

Support response target

We aim to respond to support requests within 3 business days.
